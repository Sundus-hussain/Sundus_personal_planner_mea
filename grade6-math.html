01
MONDAY · AUGUST 24

Divide Multi-Digit Numbers

Lesson 1.1
Lesson

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will divide multi-digit whole numbers accurately using standard division strategies and check whether their answers are reasonable.

📚 Lesson

  • Review multiplication facts and division vocabulary.
  • Review the relationship between multiplication and division.
  • Model multi-digit division step-by-step.
  • Explain how to estimate before dividing.
  • Discuss remainders and how to interpret them.
  • Connect division to real-world problems.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Model examples using the standard algorithm.
  • Think aloud while solving division problems.
  • Guide students through several examples.
  • Check student understanding during guided practice.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Complete guided division problems.
  • Practice multi-digit division independently.
  • Estimate answers before solving.
  • Check answers using multiplication.

📖 Vocabulary

Dividend Divisor Quotient Remainder

TUESDAY · AUGUST 25

Prime Factorization

Lesson 1.2
Lesson

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will identify prime and composite numbers and write whole numbers as products of prime factors.

📚 Lesson

  • Review factors and multiples.
  • Review prime and composite numbers.
  • Introduce prime factorization.
  • Model factor trees step-by-step.
  • Show how to write the final prime factorization.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Review prime numbers with students.
  • Demonstrate factor trees.
  • Model several examples.
  • Ask students to explain their factorization.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Create factor trees.
  • Identify prime factors.
  • Write prime factorizations.
  • Compare solutions with a partner.

WEDNESDAY · AUGUST 26

Least Common Multiple

Lesson 1.3
Lesson

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will find the least common multiple (LCM) of two whole numbers and use LCM to solve problems.

📚 Lesson

  • Review multiples of whole numbers.
  • Explain what common multiples are.
  • Introduce the least common multiple.
  • Model how to find the LCM using lists of multiples.
  • Connect LCM to real-world situations.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Create multiple lists with students.
  • Model finding common multiples.
  • Identify the least common multiple.
  • Guide students through word problems.

✏️ Student Practice

  • List multiples of given numbers.
  • Identify common multiples.
  • Find the LCM of pairs of numbers.
  • Solve LCM word problems.

📖 Vocabulary

Multiple Common Multiple Least Common Multiple LCM

Greatest Common Factor

Lesson 1.4
Lesson

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will find the greatest common factor (GCF) of two whole numbers and apply GCF to problem-solving.

📚 Lesson

  • Connect GCF to real-world situations.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Review factors with students.

  • Demonstrate how to identify the greatest factor.
  • Guide students through application problems.

✏️ Student Practice

  • List factors of given numbers.
  • Identify common factors.
  • Find the GCF of pairs of numbers.
  • Solve real-world GCF problems.
