01

DAY 1

Divide Multi-Digit Numbers

LESSON 1.1

Divide Multi-Digit Numbers

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will fluently divide multi-digit whole numbers and use estimation to check whether their answers are reasonable.

📚 Lesson

  • Review basic division facts and place value.
  • Explain long division step-by-step.
  • Model division of multi-digit numbers.
  • Discuss remainders and how to interpret them.
  • Use estimation to check answers.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Model several long-division examples.
  • Complete guided practice with students.
  • Ask students to explain each step.
  • Check common mistakes.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Complete textbook practice.
  • Practice long division independently.
  • Check answers using multiplication.

📖 Vocabulary

Dividend Divisor Quotient Remainder

Prime Factorization

LESSON 1.2

Prime Factorization

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will identify prime and composite numbers and write the prime factorization of whole numbers.

📚 Lesson

  • Review factors and multiples.
  • Explain prime and composite numbers.
  • Introduce prime factorization.
  • Model factor trees.
  • Write numbers as products of prime factors.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Create factor trees with students.
  • Model different ways to factor a number.
  • Discuss why the final prime factorization is the same.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Create factor trees.
  • Identify prime numbers.
  • Write prime factorizations independently.
