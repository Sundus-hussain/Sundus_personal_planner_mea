MONDAY · AUGUST 24

Percent Change — Day 1

Lesson 1

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will use proportional reasoning to calculate percent increase and percent decrease.

📚 Lesson

  • Introduce percent change.
  • Explain percent increase and decrease.
  • Model examples step-by-step.
  • Connect the concept to real-world situations.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Review percentages and proportional relationships.
  • Demonstrate how to find the amount of change.
  • Guide students through sample problems.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Complete guided practice problems.
  • Work on examples independently.
  • Discuss answers and explain reasoning.
