MONDAY · AUGUST 24

What Are Forces?

LESSON 1 · DAY 1

What Are Forces?

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will understand that a force is a push or a pull that can change the motion or position of an object.

📚 Lesson

  • Introduce the meaning of force.
  • Explain that forces can be pushes or pulls.
  • Use everyday examples such as opening a door and kicking a ball.
  • Discuss how forces can change the motion of an object.
  • Demonstrate push and pull forces in the classroom.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Ask students to give examples of pushes and pulls.
  • Demonstrate different forces using classroom objects.
  • Guide students in identifying the force acting on an object.
  • Discuss how a force can start or stop motion.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Identify pushes and pulls around the classroom.
  • Draw examples of a push and a pull.
  • Complete a simple force observation activity.
  • Explain how a force changes an object's motion.

📖 Vocabulary

Force Push Pull Motion
📝

Practice Questions

Complete these questions based on today's lesson.

1

2

3

4

5

Give one example of a push and one example of a pull that you use every day.

What Are Some Types of Forces?

LESSON 2 · DAY 1

Types of Forces

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will identify different types of forces and describe how they affect objects.

📚 Lesson

  • Review the meaning of force.
  • Introduce different types of forces.
  • Discuss contact forces.
  • Introduce friction as a force that can slow motion.
  • Use real-world examples to identify forces.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Demonstrate friction using different surfaces.
  • Ask students to compare smooth and rough surfaces.
  • Show examples of forces acting on objects.
  • Guide students through a force classification activity.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Identify different forces.
  • Observe friction in classroom examples.
  • Record observations.
  • Discuss how friction affects movement.

📖 Vocabulary

Friction Contact Force Motion Force
📝

THURSDAY · AUGUST 27

What Forces Act from a Distance?

LESSON 3 · DAY 1

Forces That Act from a Distance

🎯 Learning Objective

Students will understand that some forces can act on objects without the objects touching each other.

📚 Lesson

  • Review contact forces.
  • Introduce forces that act from a distance.
  • Explain gravity as a force that pulls objects toward Earth.
  • Introduce magnetic force.
  • Use demonstrations to show forces acting without direct contact.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Activities

  • Demonstrate gravity using falling objects.
  • Use magnets to demonstrate magnetic force.
  • Ask students to identify whether objects touch.
  • Compare contact forces with forces acting at a distance.

✏️ Student Practice

  • Observe a magnet attracting objects.
  • Discuss how gravity affects objects.
  • Classify examples as contact or distance forces.
  • Record observations in science notebooks.

📖 Vocabulary

Gravity Magnetic Force Distance Force Attract
📝
